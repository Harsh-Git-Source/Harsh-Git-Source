### 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist and AI Engineer** who builds autonomous AI agents, LLM applications, and data science solutions that create measurable business value.

I hold a **Post Graduate Diploma in Business Analytics (PGDBA)** — a highly selective program (QS Ranked #1 in Asia) jointly offered by **IIM Calcutta, IIT Kharagpur, and ISI Kolkata**, three of India's leading institutions in business analytics and data science.

My work spans **agentic AI systems, LLM & RAG pipelines, statistical modeling, forecasting, and end-to-end ML** — with a focus on shipping reliable, scalable solutions rather than demos.

- 🤖 I build **AI agents** that automate research, reporting, and business workflows
- 🧠 I design **RAG & knowledge systems** grounded in real data
- 📊 I turn messy data into **forecasts, predictions, and decisions**
- 🎯 I care about **business outcomes**, not just models
